Some questions do not have an answer yet

Most security work assumes the question is known: does this control exist, does that password policy hold, can this attack succeed. Research begins where those assumptions run out — a new AI feature, an unfamiliar industrial protocol, an identity model nobody has measured yet.

In those situations the honest answer is “nobody has tested this”, and the useful response is a bounded piece of work that produces evidence.

What research is not

  • It is not a penetration test with a different label.
  • It is not a compliance audit.
  • It is not a marketing claim looking for support.

If your question already has a known method, we will say so and point you at the cheaper engagement. Research is for the cases where the method itself is the work.

Who this is for

Product and engineering teams

You are building something new and need to know how it fails before your customers find out.

  • Security design review before the architecture is frozen
  • Adversarial thinking applied to AI, data and identity features
  • Findings your engineers can implement, not a list of abstractions

Industrial and critical systems

You operate connected equipment and cannot afford security theatre that ignores production.

  • Analysis across the IT and OT boundary
  • Failure modes that matter to operations, not just to compliance
  • Mitigations compatible with uptime and maintenance windows

Research and innovation partners

You need credible technical work behind a grant, pilot or tender.

  • Methodology and reproducible artifacts
  • Co-authored reports and papers
  • Results you are able to publish

How an engagement runs

  1. 01

    Scope Week 1

    We turn the question into a bounded engagement with a deliverable, timeline and price.

  2. 02

    Baseline Weeks 1–2

    Prior work, comparable attacks and your architecture, reviewed before any testing begins.

  3. 03

    Method Week 2

    A written method: what will be tested, what will not, and how results will be judged.

  4. 04

    Execute Weeks 2–8

    Research, prototype or measurement work in short increments with weekly checkpoints.

  5. 05

    Hand over or publish Final week

    Report, prototype or dataset delivered with a walkthrough; publication only with your approval.

Questions clients ask

What does an engagement cost?

Engagements start at R25 000 for a two-week scoping study. Prototypes and measurement studies are quoted after scoping, and collaborative research is usually funded jointly with a partner or grant.

Do you publish the results?

Only with your written approval. Most engagements stay confidential; collaborative research is published when all parties agree and sensitive detail has been removed.

Who owns the intellectual property?

You own the results and any code written for you, unless a joint research agreement says otherwise. We agree this in writing before work starts.

How is this different from penetration testing?

Penetration testing proves whether a known class of attack works today. Research work answers questions that do not yet have a method, a tool or an established answer.

Do you take on unsolicited research topics?

Sometimes. If the question is interesting and the output can be shared, we say so; otherwise it is quoted as a normal engagement.

Can this support a grant or tender?

Yes. We can scope to a funder's requirements and provide the methodology, timeline and deliverables needed in a proposal.

Is R&D only for large organisations?

No. The scoping study is deliberately small: it is designed so a product team or an industrial operator can answer one important question without committing to a long programme.
