Where security usually breaks down

Most South African organisations do not end up breached because they bought the wrong firewall. They end up breached because nobody is watching the alerts after hours, and the estate has quietly drifted from the configuration it was built with.

In the assessments we run, the same five patterns show up again and again:

  • Alerts are generated, but nobody owns them at 22:00.
  • Microsoft 365 was configured once during migration and never reviewed.
  • Legacy authentication, permissive OAuth consent and permanent global administrators are still open.
  • There is no evidence-backed answer to “were we breached, and what did we lose?”
  • POPIA breach notification is a legal duty with no named process behind it.

Compliance frameworks assume someone is operating security day to day. Tools do not create that capability — people, process and reporting do.

What changes when we take the operational load

MuchaTech runs security as an operating function. We agree scope and response times up front, take responsibility for detection and containment, and report against the same roadmap we are measured on.

Your team keeps ownership of the business and its systems. We carry the operational load — monitoring, triage, escalation, containment, evidence and reporting — and we tell you plainly what cannot be fixed yet, and why.

Questions clients ask

How quickly do you respond to an incident?

Response time depends on your plan: four hours for P1 incidents on Silver, fifteen minutes from Gold. Response means a MuchaTech analyst has acknowledged, triaged and started containment — not that an automated ticket was opened.

Do we have to replace our existing tools?

No. We usually work with the EDR, SIEM and Microsoft 365 licensing you already have. Where a gap is real, we say so and price it separately rather than bundling it silently.

What access do you need?

Least-privilege read access for monitoring, plus response scopes where you want us to contain threats without waiting for approval. Access is documented during onboarding and can be revoked at any time.

Does this make us POPIA compliant?

It supports your POPIA obligations — particularly the security safeguards in section 19 and breach notification in section 22. Compliance remains a joint effort with your information officer, and we do not provide legal advice.

Can you work alongside our IT provider?

Yes. Co-managed is the usual model for enterprises: we own detection and response, your provider owns endpoints, patching and change control, and escalation paths are agreed in writing.

How long does onboarding take?

Two to four weeks for a typical SME estate, depending on access approvals and how much of the Microsoft 365 tenant needs remediation before monitoring starts.

Do you support industrial and OT networks?

Yes. OT work is scoped around plant availability: passive visibility first, segmentation and playbooks second, and any change inside an agreed maintenance window.
