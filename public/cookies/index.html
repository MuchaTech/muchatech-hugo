Cookie Policy

The cookies this website sets, what they are used for, how long they last and how to control them.

Last updated 22 November 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies used on muchatech.com. It should be read together with our privacy policy, which covers personal information more broadly.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. It lets the site remember something between page loads — for example, that you have already dismissed a notice. Cookies can be set by the site you are visiting (first-party) or by a service it uses (third-party).

Cookies we use

Cookie Set by Purpose Duration Type
cookie-box MuchaTech Remembers that you accepted the cookie notice so it is not shown again 2 days Preference
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes one browser from another so visits can be counted 2 years Analytics
_ga_<container-id> Google Analytics Keeps session state for Google Analytics 4 2 years Analytics

The preference cookie is set only when you press I Accept in the banner. Analytics cookies are set by Google Analytics, which is loaded through the site’s analytics tag.

Cookieless analytics

Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights collect aggregated page and performance data without setting cookies. They record which pages are viewed and how quickly they load, not who you are.

Other third-party requests

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts. That request sends your IP address to Google even though it does not set a cookie. Fonts are requested from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.

Analytics and your choice

Our analytics may begin before you interact with the cookie banner. If you prefer not to be measured, block or delete cookies in your browser, or use a browser extension that blocks analytics scripts. The site continues to work without them.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The help pages for Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge explain how. Blocking the preference cookie means the banner will appear again on your next visit; blocking analytics cookies does not affect the site’s functionality.

Changes to this policy

If we add or remove a cookie, this table is updated and the date at the top of the page changes.
