## Some questions do not have an answer yet

Most security work assumes the question is known: does this control exist, does that password policy hold, can this attack succeed. Research begins where those assumptions run out — a new AI feature, an unfamiliar industrial protocol, an identity model nobody has measured yet.

In those situations the honest answer is "nobody has tested this", and the useful response is a bounded piece of work that produces evidence.

## What research is not

- It is not a penetration test with a different label.
- It is not a compliance audit.
- It is not a marketing claim looking for support.

If your question already has a known method, we will say so and point you at the cheaper engagement. Research is for the cases where the method itself is the work.
