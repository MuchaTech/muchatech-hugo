## Where security usually breaks down

Most South African organisations do not end up breached because they bought the wrong firewall. They end up breached because nobody is watching the alerts after hours, and the estate has quietly drifted from the configuration it was built with.

In the assessments we run, the same five patterns show up again and again:

- Alerts are generated, but nobody owns them at 22:00.
- Microsoft 365 was configured once during migration and never reviewed.
- Legacy authentication, permissive OAuth consent and permanent global administrators are still open.
- There is no evidence-backed answer to "were we breached, and what did we lose?"
- POPIA breach notification is a legal duty with no named process behind it.

Compliance frameworks assume someone is operating security day to day. Tools do not create that capability — people, process and reporting do.
