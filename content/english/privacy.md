## What we collect

**Information you give us.** When you submit the contact form we collect your name, email address, phone number, the business area and request type you select, your message and your consent to this policy.

**Information collected automatically.** Our hosting provider records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps. Analytics tools record aggregated information about how pages are used, such as page views, approximate location and referral source.

**Correspondence.** If you email or call us, we keep the details needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation.

We do not ask for, and should not be sent, special personal information (for example health, religious or biometric data) through this website.

## Why we process it

We use personal information to:

- respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote or proposal;
- deliver and support services you have asked for;
- keep the website secure, available and free of abuse;
- understand which pages are useful so we can improve them; and
- meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling that has legal consequences for you.

## Our lawful basis

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), we rely on:

- **consent** (section 11(1)(a)) for the contact form and for any marketing communication you opt into; and
- **legitimate interest** (section 11(1)(f)) for keeping the website secure and for aggregate analytics.

You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@muchatech.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened, or processing we must keep doing to meet a legal obligation.

- explain how we handle your information.

Email support@muchatech.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.
